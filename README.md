MockMate – Smart Mock Interview Platform

MockMate is a smart, skill-specific mock interview platform designed to help job seekers prepare effectively for real-world interviews. Unlike traditional platforms that rely on static question banks or automated quizzes, MockMate focuses on authentic, interactive interviews conducted by real industry experts.

The platform provides personalized performance insights, structured evaluations, and dynamic AI-generated support to help users improve consistently.


🚀 Purpose

The purpose of MockMate is to empower job seekers with:
	•	Realistic, expert-led interview experiences
	•	Targeted practice based on preferred skills or job roles
	•	Personalized feedback and performance tracking
	•	AI-enhanced insights and evaluation summaries

MockMate aims to boost confidence, readiness, and employability through consistent, high-quality practice.


🎯 Goals

MockMate is built with the following key goals:

1. Skill-Specific Interview Practice

Allow candidates to practice mock interviews in their chosen skill areas so preparation is relevant and job-focused.

2. Connect With Industry Experts

Enable real interviewers (industry professionals) to conduct sessions and provide practical insights.

3. Advanced Platform Features

Include essential capabilities such as:
	•	Slot booking for interview scheduling
	•	Requesting new interview tests in different domains
	•	Maintaining leaderboards by skill
	•	Preserving a complete evaluation history for each candidate

4. Structured Feedback

Provide expert evaluations highlighting strengths, weaknesses, and improvement areas.

5. Motivation & Engagement

Encourage users through skill-based leaderboards and progress tracking.

6. Integrated AI Support

Use AI to:
	•	Generate dynamic interview questions
	•	Analyze interview transcripts
	•	Summarize expert feedback into clear, structured reports

7. Built-In Video & Audio System

Implement a seamless real-time video/audio conferencing system using WebRTC for conducting live mock interviews.
